## Automated Content Pipeline
This workflow bridges multple APIs to autmoate content distribution across platforms.

* **Trigger:** Webhook intercepts an incoming data payload.

* **Logic:** A custom JavaScript node parses and reformats the JSON string.

* **Action:** Dispatches formatted alerts to Discord or a platform of you choosing.
